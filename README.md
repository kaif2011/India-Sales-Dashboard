# India-Sales-Dashboard

An interactive Power BI report designed to track revenue against targets, regional trends, and category-wise sales across India.

## 📌 Project Overview

The India Sales Performance Dashboard was developed to track business efficiency and revenue drivers across various states. Beyond just visualization, the project focuses on performance gaps — analyzing why total sales reached 589.65K against a 629K target.

By leveraging Power BI, I identified Maharashtra as the leading revenue-contributing state and Phones as the top-performing product category. This dashboard enables stakeholders to monitor seasonal peaks in June and September and evaluate team lead performance to drive data-backed growth strategies.

## 📁 Data Source

The dataset used contains structured sales records across India, featuring metrics such as Sales Amount, Profit, Quantity, Customer Segments, and State-wise performance data.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

The dashboard was built using the following tools and technologies:
- 📊 Power BI Desktop – Used for building the interactive report and high-level visualizations.  
- 📂 Power Query – Performed data cleaning, handled missing values, and formatted date/state columns.  
- 🧠 DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) – Developed custom measures for Total Sales, Total Profit, and Target Variance to track KPIs accurately.
- 🔗 Data Modeling – Established relationships between tables to ensure slicers (Year, Region, Manager, Category) work seamlessly.
- 📁 File Format – .pbix for development and .png for dashboard preview.

## 🚀 Business Context & Goals

### 🔸 Business Problem:
Many businesses struggle to track performance across multiple regions and product categories using raw data alone. For this project, the core challenge was identifying why the business fell short of its 629K sales target, landing at 589.65K instead — roughly a 6% gap. I built this dashboard to pinpoint revenue leakages and highlight high-growth states like Maharashtra and Uttar Pradesh.

---

### 🔸 Goal of the Dashboard:

The objective was to build an interactive dashboard that:

• Tracks Performance: Provides a clear view of overall sales (589.65K) against target (629K), along with profit (129.07K), quantity (6K), and customer count (2K).

• Regional Benchmarking: Compares performance across Indian states to identify top-performing areas and those needing intervention.

• Segment Analysis: Deep-dives into customer segments and product sub-categories to understand revenue drivers.

• Team Performance Tracking: Enables stakeholders to evaluate individual team lead contributions and identify top and low performers. 

---

### 🔸 Walkthrough of Key Visuals:

• KPI Cards (Top Section): Displays five high-level metrics — Total Sales (589.65K), Total Target (629K), Total Profit (129.07K), Total Quantity (6K), and Total Customers (2K). Insight: Gives an instant summary showing a slight shortfall against the set sales target.

• Total Sales by Sub-Category (Bar Chart): Ranks product sub-categories by revenue — Phones (105K) and Bookcases (89K) lead, followed by Copiers (70K), Chairs (69K), Appliances (45K), Tables (42K), and Accessories (34K). Purpose: Identifies which products drive the most revenue and which have growth potential.

• Total Sales by State (Bar Chart): Visualizes regional dominance, with Maharashtra (101K) and Uttar Pradesh (76K) as the top-performing states, followed by Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Delhi, Rajasthan, and Gujarat. Insight: Helps prioritize states for focused sales and marketing efforts.

• Total Sales by Segment (Pie Chart): Breaks down revenue across customer segments — Consumer (50.77%, 299.38K), Home Office (28.48%, 167.95K), and Corporate (20.74%, 122.32K). Purpose: Shows which customer segment contributes most to overall revenue.

• Total Sales by Month (Line Chart): Tracks seasonal sales performance across the year, showing a sharp peak in June and a second strong rise in September, with dips in March and July. Utility: Helps plan inventory and staffing around high-demand months.

• Sum of Sales by Team Lead (Treemap): Displays the sales contribution of each team lead. Mohit (58.75K), Suraj (56.40K), and Manu (55.63K) emerge as the top three contributors, while Ram (38.21K) trails at the bottom. Purpose: Enables transparent, data-driven performance evaluation across the sales team.

• Dynamic Filters & Slicers: Interactive filters for Year, Region (East, North, South, West), Manager (Mahesh, Ramesh, Suresh), and Category (Furniture, Office Supplies, Technology) enable deep-dive analysis.

---

### 🔸 Business Impact & Insights:

• Strategic Planning: Identified a ~6% gap between actual sales (589.65K) and the target (629K), providing a clear starting point for performance reviews.

• Regional Focus: Pinpointed Maharashtra as the highest-revenue state, suggesting that similar strategies could be replicated in lower-performing states like Gujarat and Rajasthan.

• Inventory Optimization: Trend analysis showed peaks in June and September, helping the business plan stock levels more efficiently for high-demand months.

• Product Growth: Revealed that Phones and Bookcases drive the most revenue, while Accessories show room for expansion.

• Team Accountability: Provided a transparent view of team lead performance, enabling data-driven appraisals and coaching, with Mohit, Suraj, and Manu standing out as top performers.
